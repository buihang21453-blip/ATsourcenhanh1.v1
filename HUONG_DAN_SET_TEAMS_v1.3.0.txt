CAC BUOC TEST
1. Khong mo Cheat Engine trong luc test.
2. Chay AT.exe, sau do mo PES2021.exe.
3. Vao mot tran voi cap doi bat ky va ket thuc/thoat tran.
4. Sua home/away trong at\set_teams.ini neu can.
5. Chon "Chon lai doi" trong PES.
6. Bam mot phim hoac nut tay cam de module nap lai INI.
7. Kiem tra menu co dung cap doi moi, sau do bam xac nhan.
8. Kiem tra ten, logo, cau thu, doi hinh va kit.
9. Mo at.log, tim "SET TEAMS v1.3.0" va "APPLIED".
